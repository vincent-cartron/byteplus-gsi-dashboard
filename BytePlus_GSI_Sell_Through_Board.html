Deal registration and credit, sourced and influenced

  • SourcedThe GSI brings an opportunity that is not open in the BytePlus CRM and registers it before the first BytePlus client meeting. It earns full GSI channel credit, and the registration is protected for 90 days, with one extension when progress is documented.
  • InfluencedBytePlus already works the account, and the GSI shapes the architecture, writes BytePlus into its proposal or delivers the project. The GSI is recorded as influence partner on the opportunity, and the deal counts toward the GSI target at a share agreed with sales leadership.
  • Direct sellersThe BytePlus account executive keeps full quota credit on GSI deals in their accounts, so direct sellers gain when they bring a GSI in.
  • ConflictsThe first registration with evidence wins, and the regional sales leader decides any dispute within five business days.

BytePlus already runs deal registration for direct resellers in its Partner Network, so the GSI rules extend an existing process. BytePlus Partner Network

First 90 days

Each phase ends on an exit test

Days 1 to 30

Map and select

  • Score the seven GSIs on SEA AI practice size, model alliances, industry overlap and delivery capacity in Singapore.
  • Pull every open BytePlus direct deal where a GSI already delivers, and list the partner in each one.
  • Agree the GSI target, the credit rules for sourced and influenced revenue, and the deal registration path with sales leadership.
  • Meet the Solution Architect team and pick two reference use cases per wave 1 partner.

Exit test: three wave 1 GSIs selected with a named practice lead to meet in each.

Days 31 to 60

Open and enable

  • Run a ModelArk and Seedance working session with each wave 1 AI practice, with the Solution Architect on site.
  • Agree a joint account list of 10 to 15 SEA clients per partner, built from overlap with BytePlus direct coverage.
  • Start vendor registration and the alliance agreement with procurement and legal at each wave 1 partner.
  • Give partner architects sandbox access and a short certification path.

Exit test: alliance agreements in legal review at three partners, 20 partner architects with sandbox access, and two joint PoCs scoped.

Days 61 to 90

First joint pipeline

  • Launch two joint PoCs with the Solution Architect team and agree success criteria with each client.
  • Register the first deals under the agreed sourced and influenced rules.
  • Pair a BytePlus regional leader with the executive sponsor at the lead partner and sign a one-page joint plan.
  • Start the monthly GSI forecast inside the BytePlus sales cadence.

Exit test: one alliance agreement signed, two PoCs live, five registered deals, and an executive pairing in place at the lead partner.
